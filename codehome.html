Before getting into all of that, I want to bring up my core principle in how I configure and personalize my computing experience. It's all about reducing mental friction. Friction is an opposing force that slows you down. To me, mental friction is the combination of the mental effort and the time it takes to perform an action. For example, go to the top left corner of your browser and click on the Refresh button. It refreshes the page. Now, with your hands on the keyboard, press F5. It should do the same thing. Which process took less time and effort? The keyboard is a powerful, fast, efficient way of doing most everything you ever need to do on your system, and so going forward, we'll do everything with a keyboard-first approach, and reducing mental friction will help you to be faster, more productive, and have a better time using your computer.
