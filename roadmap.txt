Goodness.fun: my personal website

This project is my homemade, hand crafted website built using my limited skills in html, js, and css.
It is a hub for my links to socials and github, and a place for me to show off my projects in github, Youtube, retro gaming, and fun.
The project is ongoing, with ideas for more pages in the works and to finish pages I already made.
The site looks good on desktop browser, as well as mobile if the device is in landscape mode.
Site is not professionally made in any way, but I deeply care about how it looks and how it feels to browse, as well as the content on it.
The biggest goals are to enjoy various forms of technology and show the world how much I enjoy it myself.


PROJECT SYNOPSIS:
- assets/: some pictures
- FiraMono/: fonts for the site
- styles.css: global styles used all around the site
  - site is loosely themed around Deadpool, a favorite superhero of mine, appearing as the top logo
  - blue background with white text, yellow links, and green when hovered
- aboutme.html: a small static page with my portrait and a paragraph about me
- codehome.html: a hub for talking about programming, and showing off finished and ongoing projects
- gallery.html: uses gallery.js to show images in a gallery in assets/ with a Previous and Next button
- if_youre_reading_this_i_love_you.html: secret
- index.html: homepage, an essay on what the site is about
- philosophy.html: will contain several thoughts of mine on things that deeply fascinate me about the world we live in
  - two spoiler principle: all text is hidden, first item reveals a title or brief description, second item reveals the full text of it
- speedrun_tool.html: educational page showing off the in-game calculator used in xg_hub, explaining some javascript that adds up times
- xg_hub.html: shows off my love for Extreme-G for N64, and contains the speedrun tool with 12 spots
  - enter in your best times on the 12 main tracks in the game, and the tool adds them up for you to get an IGT total
- footer.js: builds the footer on every page, with small logo and links to home and back to top
- gallery.js: code for referencing images in assets/ and switching them when buttons are pressed
  - image gallery could be made for different sets of images later
- header.js: builds consistent header on all pages. includes logo, my links, and nav menu to reach other pages
- table.js: builds a table in xg_hub listing the 12 tracks in the game and my best times and youtube links to recordings of those best times
- xg_script.js: for xg_hub, adds 4 buttons: add time, remove time, calculate, reset
  - igt calculator is not my own, but credit is given
